This will help you setup Git with obsidian.

First things first, create your vault! 
Then find the directory, or just make your own while you created your vault. 
Ex:  C:\Users\user\Documents\Obsidian Vaults\Git-Obsidian Tutorial

On the bottom left, near your vaults name, hit that gear wheel.
